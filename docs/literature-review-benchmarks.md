# Atmospheric Correction Benchmarks

Traditional atmospheric correction processors provide an important reference for evaluating machine learning approaches.

This review considers studies related to the performance and validation of atmospheric correction for Sentinel-2 imagery and summarizes their relation to data-driven methods.

## ACIX-II Land

Doxani et al. presented the Atmospheric Correction Inter-comparison eXercise for land applications, known as ACIX-II Land.

The study evaluated atmospheric correction processors applied to Sentinel-2 and Landsat 8 imagery. For Sentinel-2, processors included:

- Sen2Cor
- LaSRC
- FORCE
- MAJA
- SIAC
- ATCOR
- iCOR

The experiment used Sentinel-2 acquisitions distributed across multiple geographic regions. The common comparison included 79 Sentinel-2 sites.

AERONET measurements were primarily used to evaluate atmospheric variables such as Aerosol Optical Depth (AOD) and water vapour and to support the generation of simulated surface reflectance references.

RadCalNet measurements provided an additional ground-based reference for evaluating surface reflectance.

For the best-performing processors, surface reflectance uncertainties based on simulated references were approximately `0.003` to `0.01` for most evaluated bands. Validation using RadCalNet also showed relatively small differences between processor estimates and ground measurements.

Performance varied across spectral bands. Higher uncertainty was generally observed at shorter wavelengths because atmospheric scattering has a stronger influence in the visible region.

The study demonstrates that atmospheric correction performance depends on the processor, spectral band, atmospheric conditions, and reference methodology.

## Sen2Cor Validation

Pflug et al. evaluated surface reflectance generated by Sen2Cor using measurements from RadCalNet sites and dedicated field campaigns.

Sen2Cor is the atmospheric correction processor selected by the European Space Agency for generating Sentinel-2 Level-2A products from Level-1C observations.

The evaluation included:

- 21 sample days over La Crau
- 40 sample days over Gobabeb
- 4 sample days from field campaigns in Germany

The reported band-averaged relative total uncertainty was approximately:

- 7% over the bright RadCalNet sites
- 17% over the darker surfaces evaluated in Germany

The limited number of observations over the darker German sites is an important limitation of the study.

These results show that Sentinel-2 Level-2A products provide useful surface reflectance estimates but still contain uncertainty.

Therefore, a supervised model trained using Level-1C and Level-2A pairs learns to approximate a processor-generated reference rather than exact physical surface reflectance.

## Comparative Summary

| Study | Data / Reference | Method | Metrics / Results | Main Limitation / Relevance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shah et al. | 1,000 Sentinel-2 samples from India; B2, B3, B4 and B8 | Pix2Pix cGAN | SSIM 0.961; PSNR 42.14 dB; RMSE 0.0097; MAE 0.0071 | Validation split is not clearly described; demonstrates direct TOA-to-SR learning |
| Mateo-García et al. | CloudSEN12 Sentinel-2 L1C/L2A; geographically independent test locations | DTACSNet CNN | Average absolute reflectance error below 2%; approximately 1.8% discrepancy from Sen2Cor | Target is generated by Sen2Cor; relevant as an efficient L1C-to-L2A emulator |
| Doxani et al. | Sentinel-2 and Landsat 8; AERONET, RadCalNet and simulated SR | Multiple atmospheric correction processors | Best SR uncertainties approximately 0.003–0.01 for most bands | Performance depends on processor, spectral band and reference methodology |

| Shah, Raval and Divakaran | 30 peer-reviewed studies | Systematic review | Reviews RMSE, correlation and other metrics used in DL-based AC | Studies use heterogeneous datasets, targets and evaluation procedures |

## Relation to Spectra Align ML

The reviewed literature supports the investigation of supervised learning for approximating Sentinel-2 atmospheric correction.

Spectra Align ML considers the following transformation:

```text
Sentinel-2 Level-1C
        |
        v
Supervised Learning Model
        |
        v
Approximation of Sentinel-2 Level-2A
```

The study will focus on selected areas of the Caatinga biome within Ceará, Brazil.

Sentinel-2 Level-2A will be treated as a practical processor-generated reference rather than exact physical ground truth.

The project will therefore evaluate agreement between model predictions and Level-2A reflectance under the sampled geographic and environmental conditions. The results will not be interpreted as evidence of physical accuracy or generalization to the entire Caatinga biome.

Computational cost will also be considered during evaluation, particularly because the project is developed in a CPU-only environment.
